# Nutrition methodology

All rows use a 100 g cooked edible reference. `carbs_g` includes dietary fiber. Display `N/A` as unavailable.

- `reported` identifies a published composition profile. The Sri Lankan study calculated its cooked-food profiles from recipes. Digestible carbohydrate and fiber were added to express total carbohydrate.
- `estimated` identifies an approximate reference normalized from another portion or an ingredient calculation with an assumed cooked yield.
- `unknown` is reserved for rows without a usable nutrition profile.

Dosa uses the Sri Lanka Ministry of Health reference of 80 kcal per 50 g dosai, scaled to 160 kcal per 100 g. The reference does not provide the four other nutrient values. A same-recipe trial should record the cooked batch weight, piece count and oil retained to support a complete recipe estimate.

Idli uses the Indian Nutrient Databank ASC144 reference. Parata assumes a 400 g cooked batch and 15 g retained oil. Chicken Kottu assumes a 1,250 g cooked batch and uses the Parata profile as a layered-roti proxy. The lookup lists the ingredients, reference links and yield assumptions. Measured cooked yields would improve these estimates.
